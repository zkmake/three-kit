Clearer, larger row icons: download, swap in a file, live link (a pulsing dot while live), A/B and undo, which show only once a row has a swap. Tooltips say what Download saves: the swap, the source file, or a GPU readback. The preview opens right beside the panel, on the side facing the middle of the screen, level with its row, and follows the panel as it's dragged.
