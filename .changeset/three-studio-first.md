First release: mockup and model studio pages for three.js. Areas, variants and camera views from the URL (`resolveStudio`, `orbitViews`), an overlay with a draw-call readout, orbit controls, wireframe, frozen time and three-audit's console helpers (`mountStudio`, `<Studio>`, `<StudioRig>`), `saveShot(path)` for agents, and a `three-studio` CLI to receive shots and diff or montage them.
